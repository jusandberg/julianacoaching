Coaching explained

What Is Coaching?

A reflective, forward-moving conversation designed to help you access deeper clarity, make aligned decisions, and strengthen your self-leadership. Not advice — a collaborative process rooted in curiosity.

Who it supports

Coaching is for individuals who want to understand themselves more deeply and move through life with greater alignment.

Helpful distinctions

Coaching

  • Future-focused and goal-oriented
  • Centered on self-awareness, clarity, and patterns
  • Collaborative and non-directive
  • Supports action, integration, and self-leadership
  • Not clinical, diagnostic, or treatment-based

Therapy

  • Focuses on healing, mental health, and processing past or unresolved experiences
  • Delivered by licensed mental health professionals
  • May involve diagnosis, treatment, or clinical intervention

Coaching and therapy both support personal growth, but they serve different purposes.

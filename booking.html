Before you book

A small note from me.

If you're here, something in you is already asking for change, and that's enough to start. A discovery call is not a commitment. It's a quiet half hour where you can say what is going on and see how it feels to be heard.

— Juliana

Do I need to prepare anything?

No. Come exactly as you are. We start wherever you are that day.

Can we do the sessions in Portuguese?

Yes. Sessions are held in English or Português. Just mention your preference when you book, or switch anytime.

What if I'm not sure coaching is for me?

That's exactly what the discovery call is for. There's no pressure to continue afterwards.

Is the discovery call really free?

Yes. Completely free, with no follow-up obligation.

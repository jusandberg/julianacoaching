Privacy Policy.

How The U Method collects, uses, and protects information shared through this website and coaching services.

Last updated: November 2025

Privacy Policy

The U Method Coaching Inc. is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you visit this website, request an appointment, or participate in coaching services.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, phone number, location, payment details through third-party processors, and information shared through forms, booking requests, or coaching communications.

We may also collect coaching-related information such as session details, intake responses, scheduling details, and communications related to coaching services.

Basic technical information may be collected through website analytics or cookies if enabled, such as browser type, device information, and site interaction data.

How We Use Information

  • To schedule and deliver coaching services.
  • To send appointment confirmations, reminders, and service updates.
  • To respond to requests or messages.
  • To maintain client records and improve services.
  • To process payments and meet legal or administrative requirements.

We do not sell personal information or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Storage and Third-Party Services

Information may be stored or processed through trusted tools used to operate the business, such as Google Workspace, website forms, scheduling tools, payment processors, and secure digital files for notes or records.

These providers have their own privacy and security practices. We use them only to support the delivery and administration of coaching services.

Confidentiality

Coaching conversations are treated as confidential within ethical and legal limits. Disclosure may be required where there is risk of harm, illegal activity, or another legal obligation.

Your Rights

Under Canadian privacy law, including PIPEDA where applicable, you may request access to your personal information, ask for corrections, withdraw consent, or request deletion where legally possible.

To make a privacy request, email hello@theumethod.ca.

Cookies

This website may use cookies or analytics tools to understand general website use and improve the visitor experience. You can adjust cookie settings in your browser.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact hello@theumethod.ca.

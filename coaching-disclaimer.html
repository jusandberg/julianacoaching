Coaching Disclaimer

The U Method Coaching Inc. and Juliana dos Santos offer professional coaching services. Coaching is not therapy, counseling, mental health treatment, or medical advice.

Coaching is a reflective, forward-focused process that supports insight, development, and meaningful action. It does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure medical, psychological, or mental health conditions.

By participating in coaching, clients acknowledge that:

  • Coaching is not a substitute for therapy, mental health services, or medical care.
  • The coach is not acting as a psychologist, therapist, physician, or medical professional.
  • Clients are responsible for their own decisions, actions, and results.
  • Any personal or professional changes made during or after coaching remain the responsibility of the client.

If you are experiencing a mental health concern, crisis, or medical issue, please contact a qualified health professional or emergency services in your area.

Services are provided in alignment with the International Coaching Federation (ICF) Code of Ethics.
